# When does eGFR decline accelerate?

For fixed covariates, G'' = ALPHA² G h² (1 + ε) with ε = d ln h / d ln G: the decline accelerates exactly where ε < −1. Covariates: HbA1c 8 %, SBP 140 mmHg; injury rate 1× unless stated.

| UACR (mg/g) | Smallest q with acceleration | Accelerating eGFR range at q = 1.52 (trial calibration) | at q = 2.9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 2.05 | none (decelerates everywhere) | 36–110 |
| 300 | 2.30 | none (decelerates everywhere) | 37–101 |
| 1500 | 2.45 | none (decelerates everywhere) | 38–89 |

The injury rate barely moves these boundaries (it scales the injury terms together, leaving their shares of the hazard almost unchanged once k0 is small). Below the saturation point (eGFR ≈ 44 for S_SAT = 3.5) the hyperfiltration elasticity vanishes and the decline decelerates in every case; there is no terminal collapse in the model.
